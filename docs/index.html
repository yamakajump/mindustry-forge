mindustry-forge

Mindustry schematics whose numbers were measured, not claimed. Every design here was stamped into a real game on one pinned world and given the same seconds, and what you see is what the engine counted arriving at the core. Press view to see the layout, copy to paste it into your game with ctrl+v.

Beat one of these and it goes on the board. Submissions are re-measured automatically on the same bench, so nobody has to take your word for it, or mine. Source and how to submit.
